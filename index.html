Online keyboard, mouse and peripheral tester

IO is a free browser-based space for checking your devices. Select a device in the workstation above or choose a test below. Use the overview arrow to return and select another device.

Keyboard test
Check held and tested keys, key combinations, and repeat timing. Reset tested-key progress whenever you need; browser and operating-system shortcuts may remain reserved.
Mouse test
Check mouse buttons, wheel direction, movement and click intervals. Event frequency reflects browser delivery, not the hardware polling rate.
Monitor test
Inspect your display with 16 patterns, including black and white steps, color ramps, grids and alternating pixels. Use fullscreen for inspection; browser repaint timing does not measure panel response time.
Webcam test
Start a local webcam preview, choose whether to mirror it, and inspect reported track settings. Camera permission is requested only when you select Start.
Controller and gamepad test
Inspect connected controller buttons and stick axes. Choose Neutral, Xbox or PlayStation display legends; unsupported mappings use indexed controls.
Speaker and audio test
Play short test tones through the left, right or both output channels. These tones help you listen for output; IO does not measure sound or speaker quality.
Microphone test
Start a local waveform and digital amplitude display to check microphone input. Input is never played through speakers, and digital amplitude is not calibrated sound pressure.

Private device checks in your browser

IO has no analytics, uploads or recordings. Observations stay in page memory. Camera and microphone tests need your permission and a secure connection; Stop or leaving a test releases capture. Available features depend on your browser and connected devices.
